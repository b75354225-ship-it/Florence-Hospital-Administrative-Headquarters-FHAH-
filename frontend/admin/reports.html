Accessing Your Medical Reports

Patients can request approved medical reports through the FHAH healthcare support channels. All requests are handled according to hospital privacy and confidentiality procedures.

  • Submit a valid patient identification document
  • Request reports through authorized hospital offices
  • Allow healthcare staff time for verification
  • Receive reports through approved delivery methods
